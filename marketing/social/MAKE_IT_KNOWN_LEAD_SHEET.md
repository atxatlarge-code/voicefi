## 💡 Rhythmic Delivery Notes

1. **The `💥` Snare Symbol**: Represents the half-time drum snare (Beat 3). The capital bold word directly above it hits your mouth on the exact millisecond of the snare snap.
2. **The 2-Word Slide**: For lines like `Lay it on the WELCOME MAT 💥`, the word `WELCOME 💥` hits on the snare, and `MAT` slides smoothly off into the pause.
3. **Breathing Room**: You finish speaking early in every bar, leaving ~1 second of pure guitar groove before the next line.
