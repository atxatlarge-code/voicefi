THE 9 RE20 BROADCAST VARIANTS

YOKE SUSPENSION (V) + VARIABLE-D ACOUSTIC CAPSULE (A).

Hardware Benchmark: Electro-Voice RE20

The 9 vector variants below explore different geometric interpretations of the RE20’s iconic outer mounting yoke (the **'V'**), the ascending horizontal Variable-D louvers (the **'A'**), and the red tally shockmount clamp.

THE 9 BROADCAST V/A VARIANTS
VAR.01 HEAVY MONOLITH
01. Heavy Monolith

Extra-thick solid black yoke blades (V), 4 heavyweight acoustic louvers, and a solid red tally shockmount clamp with a central gold core.

assets/logo-re20-var-01.svg
VAR.02 STEPPED PYRAMID
02. Stepped Pyramid

The internal acoustic louvers step inward in width, forming an ascending triangular pyramid chevron (A) inside the cylindrical body.

assets/logo-re20-var-02.svg
VAR.03 ANGULAR STUDIO
03. Angular Studio

Razor-sharp 45° chamfered yoke arms, vertical mesh slits in the upper pop-filter dome, and horizontal lower louvers.

assets/logo-re20-var-03.svg
VAR.04 MONOLINE STENCIL
04. Monoline Stencil

Uniform heavy Swiss monoline strokes, circular pivot ring eyelets on the yoke, open airy negative space, and a bold red tally line.

assets/logo-re20-var-04.svg
VAR.05 NEGATIVE CUT
05. Negative Space Cut

Solid monolithic silhouette where the yoke channels, acoustic louvers, and A crossbar are carved out using pure white negative space.

assets/logo-re20-var-05.svg
VAR.06 WAVE DISPERSION
06. Wave Dispersion

Acoustic soundwave radiation ribs radiating along the outer edge of the yoke arms, emphasizing active vocal projection.

assets/logo-re20-var-06.svg
VAR.07 COMPACT BROADCAST
07. Compact Broadcast

High-density squat proportions optimized for 16px/32px micro-sizes. Heavyweight 3-slat Variable-D grill and thick yoke.

assets/logo-re20-var-07.svg
VAR.08 DUAL CHAMBER
08. Dual Chamber

Clear visual separation between the upper foam pop-filter dome and the lower Variable-D acoustic chamber, joined by the red clamp.

assets/logo-re20-var-08.svg
VAR.09 ARCHITECTURAL PURIST
09. Architectural Purist

Minimalist Dieter Rams geometry. Pure rectangular slats, flat studio cap, stark monolithic yoke blades, and centered red tally eye.
