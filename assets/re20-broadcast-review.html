HOW THE ICONIC RE20 BROADCAST MIC BECOMES THE DEFINITIVE V AND A.

The Electro-Voice RE20 Anatomy

The RE20 is the undisputed gold standard of studio broadcast audio. Its design contains the exact geometric ingredients for the **A and V**:

01. Outer Yoke Bracket (V) The U/V-shaped metal suspension bracket that branches up to cradle the mic body.
02. Variable-D Louvers (A) The horizontal acoustic vents tapering upwards to form the ladder of the 'A'.
03. Crossmount Band The horizontal pivot ring that connects yoke to body, acting as the 'A' crossbar.
04. Live Tally Accent Studio Recording Red (`#FF2A2A`) highlighting active broadcast capture.
THE 3 RE20 BROADCAST V/A VARIATIONS

A. The Yoke & Variable-D

Distinct outer yoke suspension arms forming the 'V' with side pivot knobs, central RE20 cylindrical body with Variable-D acoustic louvers (A), and red tally clamp.
