# 🎵 VoiceFi Audio & Sound Effect Attribution

VoiceFi bundles curated, authentic acoustic recordings for agent comedy stingers, punchlines, and developer audio feedback cues. This document provides complete attribution, origin provenance, license details, and mastering notes for all bundled sound effects in compliance with their respective open-source and Creative Commons licenses.

All bundled audio files are mastered to **44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono PCM WAV** with 0ms pre-attack latency and clean decay fades, located in [`src/voicefi/assets/`](../src/voicefi/assets/).
